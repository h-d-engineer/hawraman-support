Animals

Animals give eggs, milk and dung, have young in spring, and must be fed through the winter. In winter every animal lives indoors, so the room in the Chicken Coop, the Goat and Sheep Fold and the Gowr decides how many you can keep. Animals can be bought with walnuts (not while the pass is snowed in) or with Klash at any time, sold back, or slaughtered in autumn for preserved meat.
