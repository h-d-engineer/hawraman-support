Raids and defence

Raids are not the way to get rich in Hawraman. From the Year panel you can raid a neighbour: your guards go first, then any allies you hired. Every raid costs standing with your tribe, and neighbours remember it in hard years. Loot is walnuts, juice and fruit.

The wild comes to you: wolves and bears come down for the stores and the herd, more often in autumn and winter. Your guards and the dogs of a Kennel defend the village, together with the watchtowers, traps and walls you build.

Stars in a raid

A raid lasts 3 min. Each of three goals gives a star: reach the summit, take the banner, and break the hold (destroy 60% of the village). Three stars also give 3 Klash. Every raid costs 1 standing with your tribe.
