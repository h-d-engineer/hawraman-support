Raids and defence

Raids are not the way to get rich in Hawraman. From the Year panel you can raid a neighbour: your guards go first, then any allies you hired. Every raid costs standing with your tribe, and neighbours remember it in hard years. Loot is walnuts, juice and fruit.

The wild comes to you: wolves and bears come down for the stores and the herd, more often in autumn and winter. Your guards and the dogs of a Kennel defend the village, together with the watchtowers, traps and walls you build.

Raids on your village

Neighbours raid you too, now and then, while you are away from the game. The longer you are away, the more likely: about 8% for every hour, never more than 45%. A village is left alone for 8 hours after a raid, and a new village is left alone until its Diwan-khana reaches level 2.

Your towers, traps, walls, watchmen, dogs and the guards at home defend by themselves. Raiders can only carry off part of your walnuts, juice and fruit: at most 8% of each store at Diwan-khana level 1, up to 15% at level 5. Raiders who are driven off drop what they carried. Buildings and walls are damaged in the fight but nothing is lost: they stand again afterwards.

The raid log: what the witness saw

Nobody wrote raids down in old Hawraman, so there is no replay. When you come back, a card tells you what someone saw from a distance (who came, from which side, whether your walls held, who drove them off, what they reached and carried away), what was counted afterwards, and a sketch of the village with the way they came. Every raid, theirs and yours, is kept under Raids in the Year panel.

Answering a raid

Each raid on you can be answered once, in one of two ways:

  • Send the elders. They ask for what was taken and bring back about 30% of it, more if your standing with the tribe is good (up to 60%). With very low standing they are turned away.
  • Raid them back. You attack that same village. Answering a raid costs no standing.

It works the other way too: a village you raided may come back for you, and the witness will hear them say so.

Walls

Each wall material opens with its own Diwan-khana level: Wooden Fence at 1, Clay Wall at 2, Dry-stone Wall at 3, Brick Wall at 4 and Mortared Stone Wall at 5. You may build 85 / 100 / 120 / 135 / 150 wall pieces at levels 1 to 5, enough to ring the whole village.

  • Tap a wall, then Select connected, to choose the whole section it belongs to.
  • Stronger walls turns each piece into the next material; you pay only the difference in price.
  • Move all moves the section in one piece. Sell gives back half of what a wall cost.

Stars in a raid

A raid lasts 3 min. Each of three goals gives a star: reach the summit, take the banner, and break the hold (destroy 60% of the village). Three stars also give 3 Klash. Every raid costs 1 standing with your tribe.
