Guards, allies and heroes

There is no standing army in Hawraman. Three kinds of fighters can take part in a fight:

  • Your guards: members of your own household. Everyone fights with the shepherd's crook; each guard can learn one other way of fighting. A beaten guard comes home hurt and rests for some days.
  • Allies for hire: Hawraman helped its neighbours in their wars, and they helped in return. Fighters from allied villages come to the Meydan for pay in juice, need room at the Gathering Hearths, fight only in raids, and go home after the raid they fought in.
  • Heroes: two elders of the village, unlocked by the Hero's Hearth. They join raids, and rest after a defeat.

Hire cost is for allies at the Meydan; in summer, with enough dairy at the Summer Camp, hiring costs less. Room is the space an ally takes at the Gathering Hearths.

Heroes are upgraded with Klash, up to the level of your Diwan-khana. A beaten hero rests before the next raid; Klash can end the rest early.
