Bazaar and Klash

The Bazaar buys your fruit for walnuts or juice. Prices change every few minutes: one fruit is in demand, another is oversupplied. When the walnut and juice stores are full, you can still trade fruit for another fruit, or give some away to the neighbours to make room.

Klash

Klash are the white hand-woven shoes Hawraman is famous for: slow to make and precious. Members on the Klash task make them, the Klash Workshop weaves them, the Pir Shalyar festival gives some every year, and the best raids and wild fights reward them.
