Seasons and the year

Each season lasts one real day and has 30 game days, so a year passes in four real days. Each year has its own character: the elders can tell you in autumn what kind of winter is coming. Snow closes the pass for part of the winter: no buying or selling animals, no rice or grain from the lowlands. On the roofs, snow keeps the houses warm until it gets too heavy or starts to melt; then push it off, and roll the mud roof with the Bantleen before rain and melt make it leak.

Fast forward

When you are ready for the next day or the next season, the Year panel can let the days pass for Klash. Time really passes: the family eats, the herd is fed, collectors fill, builds and training go on, and the hurt rest. A village that is not ready for winter will feel it.

Orchards bear fruit only in their seasons, and most of it comes in autumn. The numbers are times the normal rate. In a season above ×1, whatever the orchard cannot hold goes straight to your stores, so the harvest is not lost while you are away. In winter the trees rest.

The Pir Shalyar festival

Once a year the festival gives 15 Klash: tap the season badge while it lasts, and look for glowing walnuts in the village.

Village progress

The Year panel has a Village progress button. It shows how much of your village is built for this Diwan-khana level and for the whole village, with a bar for each Shop category and a row for each kind of building: how many are built, their levels, what is being built, and what is ready to upgrade. At the bottom it lists what the next Diwan-khana level unlocks. Every kind of building counts the same, and all walls count as one.

Fast forward prices
