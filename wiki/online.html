Playing online

The game works fully on its own device, with no internet. Play online is a switch in Settings: turn it on to share the mountains with real players.

What changes when it is on

  • Real neighbours. Raid a neighbour looks for another player's village of about your level first (a copy as they last left it). If there is none, a made-up neighbour comes as before.
  • Real raids on you. Raids by other players arrive in your raid log when you come back, with what the witness saw. You can send the elders or raid them back, and they can do the same to you.
  • Your village is kept safe. A copy is sent to the game's server every few minutes, so it survives a lost phone or cleared browser.
  • Tribes. The Tribe button appears. See Tribes and score.

Nobody needs to be online at the same time. A village is left alone for 8 hours after it was raided, and villages below Diwan-khana level 2 are never offered to raiders.

Moving your village to another device

  1. On the device that has the village: Settings, Move to another device. An 8-letter code appears. It works for 30 minutes.
  2. On the other device (or browser): Settings, turn on Play online, type the code and tap Use code. The village loads there.

The village then lives on the new device; the old one stops being able to play it online.

What is stored, and deleting it

The server knows your village only by a random number. It stores the village, raids, gifts and tribe membership. It does not store your name, e-mail or anything about you, and there is no chat: nobody can type anything that others see. Village and tribe names are picked from lists.

Settings, Delete my online village removes your online village and everything stored about it. Turning Play online off stops all contact with the server. The full text is on the privacy page.

Your online name

Online, your village gets a name from the game, shown to others in raids. In a tribe you appear as a house of a place, for example "Upper house of Nodsha". You cannot type a name.
