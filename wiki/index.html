Hawraman Chronicles is a village survival game set in the stepped stone villages of Hawraman, in the Zagros mountains. You build terraces and houses, raise a household, keep a herd and fill the stores, so that your family comes through every winter.

Your first days

  1. Collect. Tap a building that has something ready (the Walnut Grove, the Pomegranate Press) to collect it. Walnuts pay for building; Pomegranate Juice pays for learning skills and hiring allies.
  2. Build and upgrade. Open the Shop. Your Builder's Huts decide how many buildings can go up at once. The Diwan-khana, the big house, decides what you may build and how many of each.
  3. Look at the year. Open the Year panel. Every season lasts one real day and has 30 game days. Winter is the test: snow closes the pass and the family lives on what it stored.
  4. Fill the stores before winter. The Stores panel shows how many days of food, fodder and firewood you have. The Dairy Room, the Kitchen, drying fruit on the roofs and the autumn slaughter all turn the year's harvest into winter food.
  5. Look after the household. In the Household panel each member works, makes Klash or stands guard. Guards protect the herd and the village, but they do not work.

Where to play

In any web browser on a computer, tablet or phone: https://hawraman-api-production.up.railway.app/. Nothing to install. Your village is saved in that browser.
