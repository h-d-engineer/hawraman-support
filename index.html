About the game

A village survival game set in the terraced mountain villages of Hawraman. Build terraces and houses, see your household through the seasons and hard winters, and follow the story mode. Free, with no ads, no purchases and no account. Available in English and Persian.

Hawraman Chronicles Lite is the current test version, shared through TestFlight on iPhone and iPad and through Google Play testing on Android.

Common questions

How do I change the language?

Use the language buttons on the title screen, or Settings. The change applies immediately.

Will my progress carry over to the full version?

No. Progress is saved only on your device, and the full version will be a separate app.

I lost my progress.

Saves live only on your device and are not backed up to any server, so deleting the app deletes them. They cannot be recovered.
