Hawraman Chronicles

Privacy Policy · Hawraman Chronicles and Hawraman Chronicles Lite · Effective date: 2026-10-09
In short: Hawraman Chronicles does not collect, share, or transmit any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no in-app purchases. It does not use third-party services or SDKs that collect information.

Network access

The game runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests. It works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage on your iPhone, iPad or Android device. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your device settings.

Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

Hawraman Chronicles is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data at all, no information is collected from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
